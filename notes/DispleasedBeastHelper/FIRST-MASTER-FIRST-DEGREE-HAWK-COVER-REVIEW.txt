PET COVER GAP
Generic RSR tank swapping was optional and limited by its saved last-horn setting. First Master did not opt into the controlled policy used by the earlier custom board assists. No proactive cover was protecting these mechanics. Fixing movement preset ownership alone would not prevent Nightmare.

CHANGES
1. Queen Hawk is an urgent priority over Corpse Flower for both BMR movement and RSR action selection. AoE center scoring cannot silently substitute the boss for an available urgent Hawk. Range, target validity and hazard avoidance still apply.
2. A Final Sting cast triggers a best-effort Smash Axe stun when the native action checks permit it and the selected target is actually Queen Hawk. It is not treated as a normally interruptible cast. Killing the add remains the primary strategy; pet cover is fallback.
3. First Master profiles now enable controlled damage sharing on every horn, independently of the generic opt-in and last-horn restriction. Healthy familiars can cover once the player is at or below 75% HP and the familiar has at least 45% HP and is at least ten percentage points healthier. Below 30% player HP, cover is an emergency option. Damage is taken back when the player is above 40% HP and the familiar is at/below 30% or at least twenty percentage points less healthy. This avoids deliberately draining a weak familiar for a healthy player, while prioritizing the player's survival in emergencies.
4. Explicit pre-cast pet cover for Acorn Bomb, Final Sting fallback and Gargoyle's Grim Fate. If necessary, try summoning a usable core beast for the protected cast; this overrides Meteor reservation for immediate survival. The current beast is not dismissed or challenged away during the cast/resolve window. Grim Fate's hold covers its follow-up hits.
5. Diagnostics identify Nightmare as an action/movement blocker and log protected-cast preparation and HP-sharing decisions. They no longer merely suggest RSR/pathing stalled when Nightmare is present.
6. Existing Meteor wave timing, ordinary Treant add priority, XP roster selection, manual entry/shops/rewards and emergency stop remain in place. First Master RSR behavior is gated to the selected First Master profiles. No live configuration JSON edited.

LIMITS / NEXT ATTEMPT
These changes are built and tested, not a verified First Degree clear. Cover depends on a living, usable familiar, action readiness and sufficient time before the hit. It can still kill a familiar in an emergency. Native resistance/range/cooldown can prevent a stun. No change can manufacture healing items; replenish manually at shops/camps as needed. First Degree's longer fights remain a sustain challenge.
Use the three-beast Legendary preset for the Legendary attempt, not the full-roster XP preset. Keep replay recording on. Inspect Queen Hawk kills/Sting completions, Snarl/Challenge logs, and whether Nightmare reaches the player during the next Treant fight.
